## From primary school teacher to dev educator

I grew up in Switzerland, where studied Sports Science and Education at University.

I spent my early career as a primary school teacher, where I practiced the art of breaking down complex topics to make them easy to understand. Eventually, I made the leap into tech. First as a designer, then as a developer, and finally as a video producer.

In a way, I came full circle. I became a developer advocate and educator, combining everything I'd learned about teaching with a deep passion for web technologies.

## Reaching developers at scale

In 2020, I joined [Tailwind Labs](https://tailwindcss.com/). I created and grew the [official YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@TailwindLabs) from **zero to over 90K subscribers, in just 18 months**. That channel became a go-to resource for developers learning Tailwind CSS, and helped establish the visual, hands-on teaching style that the community has come to expect from Tailwind's official content.

Before Tailwind CSS became the industry standard it is today, I was one of its earliest and most vocal advocates. I've probably convinced more developers to give Tailwind CSS a try than anybody outside of [Adam Wathan](https://adamwathan.me/) himself, through talks, courses, and countless passionate conversations on Twitter.

Which is how I ended up working at Tailwind Labs in the first place!

## Enterprise training

I've delivered React training workshops to **senior engineering teams at Atlassian**, helping experienced developers sharpen their skills and adopt modern patterns. My background in education means I don't just know the material. I know how to _teach_ it effectively.

## Screencast production nerd

Over the past 10+ years, I've been constantly iterating over my home screencast recording setup. What started as a webcam and a USB microphone has evolved into a carefully tuned production environment. I've accumulated a _lot_ of knowledge (and gear) along the way.

Microphones, lighting, camera settings, acoustic treatment, and everything in between. I share some of those thoughts on my [Better Dev Screencasts](https://www.betterdevscreencasts.com/) blog, but I've got a lot more to say!

If you're producing developer content and want to look and sound professional at your home or office setup, this is something [I can help with](https://cal.com/simonswiss/quick-chat).
